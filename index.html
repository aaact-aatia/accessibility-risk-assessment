This tool uses 49 weighted WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA criteria and four vendor governance questions. Scores support comparative risk analysis; they do not establish accessibility conformance. Review vendor evidence and conduct accessibility testing alongside this assessment.

2. Technical accessibility

Response guidance and scoring methodology

  • Support: sufficient evidence that the criterion is fully met (100%).
  • Partially Support: barriers exist, but functionality remains partially accessible (50%).
  • Does Not Support: the criterion is unmet or significant barriers are documented (0%).
  • Not Applicable: genuinely inapplicable or not present in the report, as specified by the original methodology (100%).
  • Not Tested: no testing reported (0%).

Critical criteria are worth 3.8125 points each; High 2; Medium 1.5; Low 1. The 49-criterion source methodology excludes 4.1.1 Parsing. The technical maximum is 90 points.

3. Vendor governance

Yes and Not Required receive full points; No receives zero. The governance maximum is 10 points.
